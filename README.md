# Early Years Parenting & Development Platform

A web application designed to help parents and carers support a child's development through practical activities, accessible guidance, and shared care tools.

This project is currently being designed and developed from scratch as both a real-world product concept and a full-stack development project.

## 🌱 About the Project

The aim is to create a simple, approachable platform for parents and carers navigating pregnancy, babies, toddlers, and early childhood.

Rather than overwhelming parents with developmental information, the platform connects early-years development with practical things families can do at home.

The project is being designed around three core ideas:

### 🎨 Activities

Age-appropriate activities that parents and carers can easily do with their children.

Activities include information such as:

* Recommended age
* Activity type
* Approximate duration
* Indoor/outdoor suitability
* Preparation required
* Materials needed
* Step-by-step instructions
* Developmental benefits
* Ways to adapt the activity

### 🌱 Learning & Development

Activities are connected to areas of early-years development, helping parents understand **why an activity is useful**, rather than simply providing a list of things to do.

Development areas include:

* Communication & Language
* Physical Development
* Personal, Social & Emotional Development
* Literacy
* Mathematics
* Understanding the World
* Expressive Arts & Design

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Shared Care

The Shared Care concept is designed to help families and carers stay on the same page.

A child's profile could eventually be shared with trusted people such as:

* Parents and partners
* Grandparents
* Nannies
* Other carers

Shared information could include activities, observations, routines, developmental progress, and important care information.
